ABout Toby Fox...

Toby Fox (full name Robert F. Fox), also known as FwugRadiation, is an American video game developer and composer. He is best known as the creator of the games Undertale and Deltarune.

About him(events):

  1. 1991:Born October 11, 1991 in America.
  2. 2008 :Under the username Radiation, he released EarthBound Halloween Hack in 2008 as part of a ROM hacking competition on Starmen.net, additionally composing the song "Megalovania" for the hack's soundtrack.[.
  3. 2015:Created Undertale, which became a breakout hit and pop-culture phenomenon.
  4. 2018 :For Undertale's fourth anniversary, Fox shared new songs from Deltarune, further announcing progression in the game's design.
  5. 2025:He didn't die but he's the goat.
